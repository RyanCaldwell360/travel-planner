# Purpose

Ryan and Lily are excited to sit down and plan a year of travel. Currently, possibilities and obligations live in their heads. They want a shared visual plan that makes opportunities, whereabouts, and PTO consequences clear whenever they revisit it.[^interview]

## Primary job

“When we sit down together on a laptop to plan the next year, help us try destinations and dates, see whether each of us can take the required time off while protecting later commitments, and compare what would need to change.”

## Desired experience — confirmed

- Exciting, visually appealing exploration rather than a laborious balance spreadsheet.
- A quick glance at individual PTO, with most attention on travel opportunities and the year plan.
- Explicit tradeoffs that support their judgment; no automated ruling on whether a shortened trip is worthwhile.
- Persistent shared memory of plans and saved alternatives, accessible from both laptops.
- A blend of map-led exploration, destination photography, and travel-journal warmth.

## Success indicators — proposed qualitative evaluation

After one planning session, both users can identify the trips being considered, each person's travel and PTO dates, the first problematic balance if any, the commitments protected, and at least one concrete alternative where available. Closing the app does not lose the plan. Returning later requires no mental reconstruction.

## Priority

The user chose **explore and compare a year of trips with clear PTO tradeoffs** over a detailed trip itinerary builder for the first implementation. Map discovery, sourced flight/lodging estimates, and weather context remain desired supporting capabilities. They should not crowd out the central workflow.
